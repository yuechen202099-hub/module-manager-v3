## Scope

- Historical reasons containing only `missing_collector_photo`, `missing_collector_info`, or `缺少采集器信息` no longer count as exceptions, regardless of order or combination.
- JSON and PostgreSQL exception lists, project progress, installer workload, review queue, review/re-photo workbench, data-center rows, and barcode archival share the same ignored-reason contract.
- Missing collector numbers and collector-only barcode gaps no longer create an open anomaly or block JSON/PostgreSQL formal approval; real meter, module, manual, and mixed anomalies remain blocking.
- New archive validation no longer generates `缺少采集器信息` for a meter group because collector evidence is terminal-level removal data rather than a required new-install meter field.
- PostgreSQL exception-list reads no longer revalidate, repair, or commit group records as a side effect.
- Mixed rows that also contain module, meter, manual, or other real exception reasons remain exceptions.
- `/barcode-maintenance/status`, `/pause`, `/resume`, and `/enqueue` are retired and return 404 for every role.
- The daily enqueue timer, background barcode worker, enqueue service, and their production runner scripts are excluded from the V3.2.26 package; deployment fails closed if an installed retired unit cannot be stopped, rechecks all units before migration, removes their unit files, and rechecks after `daemon-reload`.
- Deployment and rollback wait for Uvicorn on `127.0.0.1:8000`; a failed new-release cutover restores the previous symlink and restarts the service automatically.
- Construction live scanning, collector-inventory scanning, review-region scanning, and historical recognition results remain available.
- Existing meter number and collector number fields and constructed and unconstructed terminal merge behavior remain unchanged.
- No database migration, production data repair, OSS write, camera change, or client-platform request is included.
- This candidate has not been deployed to production.
- V3.2.25 remains the immutable rollback target.
